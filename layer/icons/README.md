The in-game panel's icons: Google's Material Symbols
(https://github.com/google/material-design-icons), rounded, filled, weight
400, under the Apache License 2.0 (`svg/LICENSE`). `svg/games-recent.svg`
puts two of them together (a controller and a history clock).

`dev/icons.sh` makes the `.a8` files from `svg/`: each is the icon's coverage,
one byte a pixel, at 16, 20, 24, 32, 40 and 48 pixels.
